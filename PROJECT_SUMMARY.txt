PROJECT OVERVIEW
----------------
A complete implementation of NEAT (NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies)
solving the LunarLander-v2 environment, with emphasis on VISUALIZATION and
EDUCATION over raw training speed.

TOTAL CODE: ~2,344 lines
FOCUS: Making NEAT's evolution process easy to understand

KEY FEATURES
------------
✅ Comprehensive training system with detailed logging
✅ Beautiful network visualizations (color-coded weights)
✅ Evolution animation (GIF showing network growth)
✅ Statistical plots (fitness, diversity, complexity)
✅ Interactive demo mode
✅ Full documentation with examples

CONFIGURATION HIGHLIGHTS
------------------------
Population Size: 150 (larger for harder task)
Target Fitness: 200.0 (LunarLander solved)
Inputs: 8 (x/y position & velocity, angle, angular velocity, leg contacts)
Outputs: 4 (discrete thrusters via argmax)
Activation: tanh (smooth, pedagogically clear)

Mutation Rates (tuned for observable evolution):
  - Node addition: 30%
  - Connection addition: 50%
  - Weight mutation: 80%

EXPECTED RESULTS
----------------
Training Time: 1-3 hours
Generations to Solve: 200-400 (typical)

Initial Performance:
  Gen 0-50: Negative rewards and frequent crashes

Mid Training:
  Gen 50-150: Hovering behavior emerges, scores creep toward 0-100

Final Performance:
  Gen 150-300: 200+ average reward (SOLVED!)

Network Evolution:
  Start: 12 nodes (8 inputs + 4 outputs), sparse connections
  End:   15-20 nodes, 30-50 connections
